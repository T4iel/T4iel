Understanding the Student Interdisciplinary Journey Management System (SIJMS)

The Student Interdisciplinary Journey Management System (SIJMS) is designed to support and model how interdisciplinary education pathways are conceptualized, managed, and experienced by students at Ahmedabad University and worldwide.

While higher education institutions are evolving toward information-driven and evidence-based curricula, students often face challenges navigating complex course requirements to reach their graduation goals. SIJMS bridges this gap by providing data-driven recommendations, clear course prerequisites, and transparent academic mapping.

Core Objectives

Key pillars guiding the design, development, and deployment of SIJMS

01

Student Journey Mapping

Analyze the complexities, decision-making processes, and challenges of students' interdisciplinary learning pathways toward personalized graduation goals.

02

SRS & Stakeholder Co-Design

Extract, document, and specify system requirements (SRS) through cross-disciplinary workshops involving students, faculty, CDC, and admissions officers.

03

System Co-Design

Co-design the SIJMS platform through iterative consultations to ensure it effectively resolves identified academic and advising needs.

04

Prototype & DBR Refinement

Develop working prototypes, validate modules with stakeholders, and refine SIJMS using Design-Based Research (DBR) methodology.

05

EdTech Portal Integration

Integrate SIJMS into LMS and Ahmedabad University's AURIS portal for long-term sustainability, reusability, and cross-institution adoption.

06

AIED & TEL Research

Address pressing open research questions in AI in Education (AIED) and Technology-Enhanced Learning (TEL&T) from a system design perspective.

Why This Matters

The SIJMS portal brings students, course pathways, and graduation goals into one easy-to-use platform. By clarifying course connections and skills gained, we make academic planning transparent, worry-free, and empowering for everyone.
