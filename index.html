T4IEL Lab | SIJMS

Studen Interdisplinary Journey Management System

Project Overview

The importance of designing and developing the SIJMS lies in its potential to explore and support — how IE, especially from students’ individual journey navigations, is conceptualized, managed, and experienced at Ahmedabad University and in other higher education institutions worldwide, where IE is already introduced or being planned. Many higher education institutions are trying to reshape their IE-based curricula to be more information-driven, evidence-based, timely, and focused. However, in the context of IE-based learning pathways, students and their parents face difficulties in deciding the educational pathway navigation to achieve their end-of-graduation goals.

Objectives

01

Understand the complexities, challenges, and decision-making processes of students’ IE journeys concerning personalized end-of-graduation goals by involving students, teachers, curriculum designers, admissions officers, CDC officers, and institutional leaders

02

Systematically extract, document, and specify the requirements as Software Requirements Specification (SRS) of the envisioned system through cross-disciplinary participatory approaches, which include workshops and focused groups comprising students, teachers, and other educational stakeholders.

03

Co-design SIJMS through iterative consultations with students, teachers, and other stakeholders, ensuring it successfully addresses their needs and requirements mentioned in the SRS.

04

Develop a working system prototype, validate each module with relevant stakeholders, and iteratively refine SIJMS by following a Design-Based Research (DBR) methodology before finalizing a minimum viable state-of-the-art product.

05

Explore potential scalability and integration strategies for SIJMS into existing EdTech tools and systems within the host institution (e.g., Learning Management Systems (LMS) and Ahmedabad University’s AURIS portal), ensuring long-term sustainability, reusability, and broader adoption to other IE institutions.

06

Address a few pressing open research questions of AI in Education (AIED) and TEL&T from a system design and development viewpoint across different DBR phases.
