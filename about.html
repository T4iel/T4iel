About T4IEL Lab

Technology of, for, in, and across Interdisciplinary Education Laboratory

Our Mission

The T4IEL Lab at Ahmedabad University develops intelligent, student-centric web systems and visual analytics tools designed to simplify complex interdisciplinary course choices and academic pathways.

Our flagship project, the Student Interdisciplinary Journey Management System (SIJMS), provides evidence-based guidance for students, helping them model their educational trajectories, fulfill prerequisites, and reach their graduation goals with clarity and confidence.

How We Support Academic Stakeholders

Empowering students, faculty, and university leadership through data-driven advising tools

01

Enhancing Student Learning

Supports students in making informed, worry-free decisions regarding their courses, interdisciplinary study paths, and career trajectories.

02

Empowering Faculty & Designers

Provides teachers and curriculum designers with actionable insights into how students engage with course requirements to continually refine offerings.

03

CDC & Placement Support

Assists Career Development Cells (CDC) and placement offices in aligning student skill profiles with suitable industry and career opportunities.

04

Institutional Leaders & Admissions

Delivers data-driven insights for institutional decision-making, student recruitment strategy, and enrollment management.
