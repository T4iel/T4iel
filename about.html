About the Lab

The Technology of, for, in, and across Interdisciplinary Education Laboratory (T4IEL Lab) at Ahmedabad University builds simple web tools and charts to help students understand their courses, pick the right classes, and plan their studies successfully.

How We Help You

The proposed SIJMS will be designed comprehensively to model and represent IE-based journeys and incorporate evidence-based insights for students’ multifaceted and complex trajectories. The following are some primary aspects that reflect the importance of SIJMS and its overall importance.

Enhancing Students’ Learning Experience

The SIJMS will support students in making informed decisions regarding their courses, study paths, and career trajectories.

Empower Teacher and Curriculum Designers

The SIJMS will provide teachers and curriculum designers with valuable insights into how students engage with the curriculum, enabling them to refine course offerings and improve educational outcomes.

CDCs and Placement Support

The SIJMS will assist CDCs and placement cells in guiding students toward suitable career paths and opportunities based on their academic interests.

Supporting Institutional Leaders and Admissions Officers

The SIJMS will provide valuable data-driven insights for institutional planning and decision-making, particularly in areas related to student recruitment and enrollment management.

Our University Home

We are located at the School of Engineering and Applied Science, Ahmedabad University. Our Virtual Lab is a place where university professors, research assistants, and student developers work together to build helpful education tools that support student success.
